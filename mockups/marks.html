A mark of its own

Today Demand Desk wears Bench's 3×3 square, like every app in this look. Four marks that say something about this app, drawn in the same dot-and-hairline language. Each is shown large, in the title bar on Paper and Backlit, and at favicon size. ▶ Working plays what it does while something is in progress (a save, reading your notes folder); ✓ Saved plays the moment a save lands. Bench keeps the square.

Today — Bench's square

The shared mark: five lit dots on a 3×3 grid; a snake runs through it while working.
A

Route

Dev → QA → Prod: two stations reached and the go-live ring still ahead — the transport rail the app is built around.recommended

Working: a dot travels the route and each station it passes lights. Saved: it lands in the Prod ring, which fills green for a moment.

B

Docket

A demand as a ticket stub — a dotted perforation, a punch in the stub, two lines of request.

Working: the perforation lights hole by hole. Saved: the punch turns green.

C

Monogram

A D drawn in eight dots — the plainest way to say whose window this is.

Working: a light runs round the letter. Saved: the whole D brightens once.

D

Queue

Three demands in a list, the top one open (blue) — the work waiting on your desk.

Working: the light moves down the list. Saved: the top demand ticks green.

My pick is A, Route. It's the one only Demand Desk could have: the go-live route is what the app tracks every day, it reads at 16 px, and it's the same drawing as the transport rails inside. The mark would replace the square in the title bar and become the browser-tab icon. The other Desk tools (Team Desk, Effort Desk, Review Desk, Monitoring Desk) would each get their own when we next touch them.
