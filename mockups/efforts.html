What this mockup proposes
  1. Log from anywhere — ⏱ Log time or T. Pick the day, the hours (2, 1.5, 2h30, 90m), an activity and what you did. The bar shows how full that day already is.
  2. A timer in the status bar: start it on a demand, stop it and the hours are filled in for you.
  3. Effort tab on every demand — how the hours fell across its life, with the status line above and the running total against an estimate. Hours logged while it was in Bug count as rework automatically.
  4. Effort workspace (new rail icon) — 26 weeks in dots, where the hours went (by demand · epic · activity · theme), the activity mix, a week sheet you can copy into your timesheet, estimate vs actual, rework cost, your weekday rhythm, and things worth noticing.
  5. Not every hour is a demand: meetings, admin, support and learning are buckets too, so your days add up.
  6. In the real app, Today would also show “logged today 3.5 / 8 h” and nudge you about days with no log.
  7. Data: one list in your JSON — efforts[{date, hours, demand or bucket, activity, note, subtask}]. Nothing else changes.
